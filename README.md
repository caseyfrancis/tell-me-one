# Tell Me One

Pull a card, tell a story. A free conversation game for generations around the table.

## How to play

Open the game on one phone or tablet and pass it around the table.

1. Add the players and tag each one by age: little kid, big kid, grown-up, or grandparent.
2. Pick the kinds of cards you want in the bowl.
3. Choose a mode:
   - **Around the table**: pass after every card.
   - **Interview mode**: one person answers 3 questions in a row, then pass.
4. Every card says who answers. Some cards get handed to a grown-up, a grandparent, or a kid.

Tap **Keep this story** on any answer worth remembering. The saved list is waiting at the end.

## The cards

| Shape | Category | Who answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ▲ | Roots | Family stories from grown-ups and grandparents |
| ☆ | Wonder | Everyone, imagination questions |
| ○ | Mirror | Everyone, noticing each other |
| ◇ | Deep | Big kids and grown-ups |
| □ | Play | Kids lead, do something instead of saying it |
| ☾ | Kids Only | Kids answer |
| ♡ | Wild | Your choice |

## Suggest a question

Use **Suggest a question** at the bottom of the game's start screen.
